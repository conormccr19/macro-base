Questions

The short version.

Still curious? The full launch announcement will cover platforms, pricing and the roadmap in detail.

When does MacroBase launch?

We're targeting a public v1.0 launch on 1 January 2027. Early access invitations go out in waves before then, starting with the waitlist.

What will it cost?

A generous free tier will cover everyday tracking. A single low-cost Pro plan unlocks advanced trends, exports and integrations. Early access members get founder pricing for life.

Which platforms are supported?

v1.0 ships as a fast, installable web app that works on desktop and mobile. Native iOS and Android builds are on the roadmap right after launch.

Where does my data live?

In the EU, encrypted in transit and at rest. You can export everything at any time and permanently delete your account in one click — no emailing support required.

Is it just another calorie app?

No. MacroBase joins nutrition, training and recovery so the numbers actually explain each other. It's a base layer for decisions, not a food diary with a chart bolted on.
